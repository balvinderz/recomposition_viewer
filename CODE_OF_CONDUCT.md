# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone. Be
respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and accept
constructive feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of
another person's private information are not tolerated.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions or participation that
violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository
owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
